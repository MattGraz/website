Philosophy

01

Empower Others

I believe in never being a blocker to someone else getting their job done. Whether it's helping a colleague solve a Python problem or breaking down barriers between teams, empowering others to succeed is always a worthwhile undertaking.

02

Lead with Integrity

Play by the rules. Give credit where credit is due. Own and fix your mistakes — and don't repeat them. These simple principles guide every decision I make.

03

Build Bridges, Not Walls

Much conflict arises from a lack of perspective. I pride myself on maintaining the big picture and helping teams with sometimes-conflicting objectives find common ground and deliver value together.

04

Health & Wellness

A person's capacity to empower others and lead effectively grows exponentially with the effort allocated to their own wellness — mentally, physically, and emotionally.

05

Continuous Learning

Whether cracking open an economics book, watching videos demystifying neural networks, or building contraptions with a Raspberry Pi, I simply love learning.
